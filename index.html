How it works

  1. Step 1

    Plan once.

    Your meals are blocks, with the calories and protein already worked out.

  2. Step 2

    Tick what you ate.

    One tap per meal. Nothing to weigh or search.

  3. Step 3

    Weigh in weekly.

    A four-week average, so one heavy morning counts for little.
