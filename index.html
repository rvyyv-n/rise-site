How it works

  1. Step 1

    Plan once.

    Your meals are blocks, with the calories and protein already worked out.

  2. Step 2

    Tick what you ate.

    One tap per meal. Nothing to weigh or search.

  3. Step 3

    Weigh in weekly.

    A four-week average, so one heavy morning counts for little.

Today

Your day in time order, with a line for now. Swap a meal, add one, or log something off-plan by name and calories.

Try it: tick Snack

Weight

Rise suggests. You decide.

When the trend drifts from your target, Rise says what to change and why. It never changes your plan on its own.

Plan and recipes

Targets for each phase, a grocery list you can tick off in the shop, and the meals you make often.
