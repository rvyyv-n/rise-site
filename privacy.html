Private by design

Privacy

Your plan, ticks and weights stay on your device.

This site
This site has no cookies, no analytics and no trackers. Cloudflare serves it and keeps standard request logs.
The app
The app keeps everything in your browser's storage.
The network

Only two things use the network:

  • The update check asks GitHub for the latest version number.
  • Web reminders, if you turn them on, store a push address, your timezone and your meal times. They store no diet data.
Your data
Export or delete your data from Settings.

In short

It all stays on your device.

Your plan, ticks and weights live in your browser's storage, not on a server.

No account.

Nothing to sign up for.

No analytics.

No cookies and no trackers, on the app or on this site.

It works offline.

Install it from the browser menu and it runs without a connection.

Export or delete it.

Both are in Settings. Each copy keeps its own data.

Open source.

Every line is public, under the MIT licence.
